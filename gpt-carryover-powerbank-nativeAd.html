Good question — here are the key things to consider:

Capacity (mAh): 10,000mAh gives roughly 2–3 phone charges; 20,000mAh gets you 4–5 and can fully charge a laptop once. All five options above sit at 20,000mAh.

Wattage: Look for 100W+ USB-C output to charge laptops at full speed. These five range from 130W to 145W. Below 45W, many laptops charge slower than they drain.

Ports: Some packs offer only USB-C; others mix in USB-A. If you charge multiple devices at once, check the port count — these range from 3 to 4 ports depending on the model.

Extras: Some models include wireless charging pads or built-in kickstands, which can be handy for on-the-go use.

Airline rules: The FAA limits carry-on power banks to 100Wh. All five fall well under that threshold (72–74Wh), so no special approval is needed.
